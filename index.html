04 Straight answers

Before you drive over.

Do I need an appointment?
No sir. We're open Monday through Friday, 7:00 to 3:00. Bring the part on by. If it's a big one — or it's coming in on a trailer — give us a ring first at (903) 597-6685 so we can make room for you.
Is my job too small for you?
There's no such thing here. Folks come in for six small welds and leave in ten minutes. A little job done right is how we've kept the big ones coming for ninety-seven years.
What metals do you work with?
Steel, aluminum, stainless and brass — including heliarc (TIG) work. Not sure what you've got in your hand? Bring it by and we'll tell you.
Do you sell metal, or just do the welding?
Both. We keep one of the most versatile metal inventories in the area right here on the racks. Walk in, tell us what you need, walk out with it — and if you'd rather we cut, shear, brake or bend it for you, we'll do that too.
Can you build something custom?
That's most of what we do. Bring a drawing, a photo off your phone, or the broke original. Fabrication, repair, machining, pipe and tubing bending — big or small.
What does it cost?
Depends entirely on the metal and the hours. Call (903) 597-6685 or come by with the job and we'll tell you straight before anybody strikes an arc.
Where exactly are you?
1716 E Erwin Street, Tyler, Texas 75702 — the brick building on East Erwin with LEW'S on the front. Pull it up on the map.
